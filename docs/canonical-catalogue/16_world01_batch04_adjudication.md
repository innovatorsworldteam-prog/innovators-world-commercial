## Critical cases (evidence-based)

### Case 1 — Product Manager vs Technical Product Manager (`#57` vs `#61`), and AI Product Manager (`#62`)

| Lens | Product Manager | Technical Product Manager | AI Product Manager |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Title/market | PM (T2, PMI) | Technical PM (T3) | AI PM (T3) |
| B. Occupational identity | Product ownership/strategy | Product mgmt for technical products | Product mgmt for AI products |
| C. Competency/skill | Product strategy, discovery, roadmap | PM + technical depth | PM + AI domain |

**D. Strong enough for separate careers?** NO. #61 and #62 are adjectives over the single product-management function (#57) — the standing rule is **do not multiply product careers per technology/technicality**. Recommendation **ALIAS** #61 and #62 into #57 (seed 5/5). Different titles, same occupational identity — **not** different careers.

### Case 2 — Product Manager vs Product Owner (`#57` vs `#63`)

| Lens | Product Manager | Product Owner |
|---|---|---|
| A. Title/market | PM (T2) | PO (T3, Scrum) |
| B. Occupational identity | Product ownership/strategy (outcome) | Team-level backlog ownership (agile) |
| C. Competency/skill | Strategy, discovery, roadmap | Backlog, sprint priorities, team-level acceptance — a team-level manifestation of #57 |

**D. Strong enough for separate careers?** NO. PO is the team-level Scrum manifestation of product management — same function, smaller scope. Recommendation **ALIAS** #63 into #57.

### Case 3 — Technical Program Manager vs IT Project Manager (`#58` vs `#59`)

| Lens | Technical Program Manager | IT Project Manager |
|---|---|---|
| A. Title/market | TPM (T2) | IT PM (T2) |
| B. Occupational identity | Cross-team technical **programme** execution | Scoped IT/delivery **project** delivery |
| C. Competency/skill | Programme planning, cross-team coordination, dependencies | Project planning, scope/schedule/budget, risk (PMI) |

**D. Strong enough for separate careers?** YES — the programme-to-project level distinction is a real function split, both distinct from product (#57, outcome). The three form a product/programme/project hierarchy. Recommendation **APPROVE** #58 and #59 as distinct careers (**attention case: Technical Program Manager vs IT Project Manager**).

### Case 4 — Solutions Architect vs Software Architect (`#65` vs `#68`), and Enterprise Architect (`#69`)

| Lens | Solutions Architect | Software Architect | Enterprise Architect |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Title/market | Solutions architect (T2, TOGAF) | Software architect (T3) | Enterprise architect (T2, TOGAF) |
| B. Occupational identity | End-to-end solution design | Application-level design | Organisation-scale design |
