A few things to know.

Can I purchase these products now?

This is a preview collection. Build and download an enquiry list to discuss pricing, production and availability with Innovators World. It does not place an order or take payment.

What materials will be used?

Each product lists its proposed materials. Final composition, dimensions, print method and care instructions will be confirmed against supplier samples before sale.

Are these finished product photographs?

The visuals are AI-generated product mockups based on the current Kiivo and Leera references supplied for this collection. They illustrate proposed designs, not manufactured stock. Final artwork, materials and appearance will be confirmed through supplier samples.

What about delivery and returns?

Shipping destinations, delivery times and the applicable returns policy will be supplied with the final offer before any payment.
