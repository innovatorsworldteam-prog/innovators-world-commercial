What is Innovators World?

Innovation is not a subject. It is a way of seeing the world.

Innovators World gives young people the space, tools and experiences to develop the mindset behind meaningful innovation.

Observe

Notice what others overlook. Learn to look closely before deciding what something means.

Question

Challenge assumptions, ask better questions and explore possibilities beyond the obvious answer.

Create

Turn ideas into something real, test it, improve it and learn from what happens.
